# Prism Plaza

Browser gacha game, built step by step. Everything lives in one self-contained file: `index.html` (HTML + CSS + JS, no build step, no libraries). Open it in a browser to play.

## How we work
- The owner adds features in small batches. Keep it a single file unless asked to split it.
- Keep the visual style: dark frosted-glass UI (tokens in `:root`). Font: **Cinzel Bold** (the owner's file, embedded as base64 in `@font-face`) for all UI text via `--display`/`--body`; Nunito (`--read`) only for longer readable text (descriptions, `.tiny`, paragraphs, inputs, chat).

## What exists
- **Main screen (home):** full-screen wallpaper (default "Pearl Tide" or the splash art of an owned character/skin, always shown whole: uploaded art full height with a blurred fill, chibis standing) with HUD pieces floating over it: framed avatar with level badge + name + title (opens the profile), Wallpaper and **Hide UI** buttons (hides everything except the wallpaper; any tap or Esc brings it back), stamina and currencies, Mail/Settings/Admin, a world-chat ticker (bots), a limited-summon promo card, minimalist round icons (Daily Login, Daily Spin, Events, Ranking, Friends / Shop, Quests, Bag, Team), an Enter Plaza portal, bottom tabs (Characters, Skins, Pets, Collectibles, Collection), a UR pity card and the Summon button. Red diamond dots mark things to do. Events, Quests, Friends, Ranking, Shop, Bag and Team are still templates.
- **Character screen:** full-screen (`openCharacters`): roster of round avatars on the left, splash art shown whole and full height (never cropped; a blurred copy fills the sides), tabs Home (stars, skins, status, wallpaper / plaza look; copy counts are deliberately not shown), Bond (gifts, bond level, marriage) and Character Info (profile). A row of badges sits under the name. Hanger button switches outfits (skins), eye button hides the UI to view the art. No combat stats by design.
- **Currencies:** gold coins, pearls, summon tickets (1 ticket = 1 pull; buy them for 3 pearls each from the ticket "+" button, `openBuyTickets`), stamina recovery bottles (+60 stamina, used from the stamina pill, `openStamina`), rings (for marriage; 300 pearls each, `RING_PRICE`). Skins can be bought for 60 pearls each (`SKIN_PRICE`, `buySkin`) from the character screen's outfit preview or the collection.
- **No elements:** characters/pets have no element; old saves have `element` stripped on load. The character screen and Character Info don't show where a character is found or its skin list.
- **Stamina:** `S.stamina {v, t}`, max 240, +1 every 6 minutes (`stamina()` regenerates on read, `spendStamina(n)`). Catching an egg in the plaza costs 20; admin Wallet tab has test buttons.
- **Daily spin:** `S.spin {last}`, one free spin a day on an 8-segment wheel (`openSpin`, `SPIN` weights): 2,000 coins 25%, 1 pearl 20%, stamina bottle 15%, 3 pearls 12%, summon ticket 12%, 5,000 coins 10%, 5 pearls 5%, marriage ring 1% (jackpot). The reward is granted when the spin starts.
- **Daily login:** `S.login {last, streak}`, one summon ticket per day on a 7-day calendar (`openLogin`); opens on boot when unclaimed.
- **Profile:** `S.profile {avatar, frame, title, card, showcase[4], uid}`, opened from the home avatar (`openProfile`). Namecard banner with avatar, name, title and stats; a showcase of up to 4 owned characters; tabs to pick the avatar (art of any owned character/skin, or your plaza chibi), avatar frame (Silver Ring, Gilded Crest, premium Prism Halo), title (6 styles from plain to animated prism glow) and namecard (5 designs incl. premium Prism Aurora, or an owned character's art). Unlocks are rules in `UNLOCK` (daily login, own 3 characters, own a UR, a 6-star character).
- **Bond:** `S.bond {[charId]: {xp, married}}`, levels 0–10 (`BOND_XP`, `bondLv`). Gifts (`GIFTS`, bag in `S.gifts`): Lantern Sweets +40 (1,000 coins), Pearl Bracelet +150 (4,000), Star Plushie +400 (10,000); with none in the bag, Give buys one with coins. At level 10 you can propose with 1 ring (`askMarry` → `marry`), which plays a hearts animation over the character's splash art shown full screen and unlocks the character's **wedding skin** if one is set (admin Game pool → "Wedding skin" on a character; left empty for the owner to fill).
- **Badges:** per-character crystal crests (`BADGES`, SVG by `badgeSVG`: metal diamond frame, faceted gem, emblem; top tiers get wings, sparkles and an animated glow): Collected, Rising Star (3★), Max Stars (6★, gold glow), Close Bond (Lv 5), Soulbound (Lv 10, gold glow), Eternal Vow (married, prism glow). Hover, focus or tap shows a tooltip with the requirement (`data-tip`, shared tooltip `showTip`). Shown on the character screen (locked ones greyed) and earned ones on profile showcase cards.
- **Username:** editable in the profile (pencil next to the name) and in Settings, max 16 characters.
- **Pets:** type `pet`, drawn by `drawPet` (dragon, phoenix/bird, fox, cat, bunny, owl; look `{kind, body, accent, belly}`, editable in admin as "Pet look"). Samples: UR Aurora Dragon and Ember Phoenix; SSR Maple Fox, Pearl Cat, Mochi Bunny, Lantern Owl. Pets never come from banners; they hatch from eggs.
  - `S.pets {frags:{ssr,ur}, eggs[], inc[3]}`. The 3-star summon drop is 500 coins (65%) or an SSR (27%) / UR (8%) egg fragment (`FRAG_SSR`, `FRAG_UR`); 10 fragments combine into an egg.
  - Eggs also spawn around the plaza (up to 3); walking into one catches it for 20 stamina (`EGG_STAMINA`), 15% are UR.
  - 3 incubators; SSR eggs take 30 min, UR eggs 2 h (`EGG`). Hatch instantly for pearls: 1 per 2 minutes left, min 2 (`hatchCost`). Hatching picks a random pet of that rarity, with the same star/duplicate rules as summons (`grantEntry`), and plays a crack-and-reveal animation (`showHatch`).
  - Home "Pets" tab opens the Hatchery / My pets screen (`openPets`); a red dot shows when something is ready.
- **Collectibles:** item type and collection tab exist; content arrives in a future update.
- **Rarities:** UR (purple/pink/turquoise) and SSR (light orange). The lowest tier is a 500 gold coin drop instead of a character.
- **Summons:** paid with summon tickets when you have enough (1 per pull, `payFor`), otherwise 3 pearls per pull, 30 per ×10.
  - UR 2% per pull, guaranteed by pull 50. SSR 5.1%, at least one every 10 pulls.
  - Limited banner: one featured UR, 50/50; losing gives a random permanent-banner UR and guarantees the featured UR next time.
  - Permanent banner: every UR flagged `standard` has an equal chance.
  - Each banner has its own pity (`S.gacha.limited`, `S.gacha.standard`).
  - Duplicates: first copy = 0 stars, each duplicate +1 star up to 6, then each extra copy = 15 pearls for a UR or 2 for an SSR (`OVERFLOW_PEARLS`, `overflowOf`).
- **Summon screen:** full-screen (`openSummon`): Back/?, tickets and pearls top-right, banner tabs on the left (Permanent "Pearl Wish", Limited with timer), the banner's character art full screen and uncropped when it has uploaded art (permanent banner: portrait chips pick which UR is shown), otherwise a chibi lineup, the title with "Summon N more times / Guaranteed UR", dark backdrops behind the title and banner-tab text plus an edge vignette, Rates / History / Shop buttons, a **Skip** checkbox (`S.settings.skipPull`, jumps straight to the results) and Summon 1 / Summon 10 buttons. Rates, pity and history open in a modal (`openRates`).
- **Pull animation:** shooting star, charge orb, UR/SSR stamp, full-screen 9:16 splash reveal with layered canvas sparkles in the rarity colors. Taps can't skip the intro (Skip button does).
- **Character art:** 9:16 PNG splash art, uploaded in the admin panel (scaled to 1600px tall). Without art, a chibi is drawn from the entry's "plaza look".
  - **Star splash arts:** characters can also have a 3★ and a 6★ art (`entry.art3`, `entry.art6`, IndexedDB keys `art3:<id>` / `art6:<id>`; admin form → "Star splash arts"). Missing ones fall back to the base art. They unlock at those stars; the player picks which one shows on the character screen's Home tab ("Splash art" chips, `S.artPick[id]`); the default is the highest unlocked. `mediaArt(e)` returns the chosen art, `artFrom(e.art)` the base one (used by the admin form). Backup/import carries all three.
- **Admin panel:** Game pool (add/edit characters, skins (a shirt button on each character row and "Skins of X → Add a skin" in the character form start a new skin pre-filled for that character, `newSkinFor`), pets, collectibles, items; there are no weapons, old weapon entries become items), Banners (limited featured UR + end date, permanent UR list), Icons (replace any icon with an image), Wallet & summons (test balances incl. rings and bottles, reset daily login/spin, reset pity, add rings/gifts, max or reset bonds).
- **Plaza (world):** 2.5D isometric lantern-festival town at night (canvas, all art procedural): warm stone paving with baked lamp light, tiled-roof houses with glowing lattice windows, lantern strings, street lamps, market stalls, red carpets, maple trees, a pond with floating lanterns, fireworks and drifting leaves/confetti. Two ground levels: the Tea Terrace is one level up (`hgt`, `stairs`, `heightAt`), and walkers use BFS pathfinding (`findPath`) so they take the stairs. Five areas (Lantern Square, Tea Terrace, Night Market, Maple Garden, Firework Stage) with a teleport map (M), minimap, area titles and a people list. Other players are bots; there is no real multiplayer yet.
- **Chibi style:** `drawChibi` draws big-head chibis (outlined, big anime eyes, pointed fringe, 5 hair styles); it's used in the plaza and for card portraits/splashes when an entry has no uploaded art.

## Data
- Game state: `localStorage` key `prismplaza.v1` (object `S`).
- Images (splash art, custom icons): IndexedDB database `prismplaza`, store `media`.
- Admin panel → Game pool → "Backup and import" copies/pastes all of it as JSON.

## Likely next steps
- Real multiplayer needs a server (e.g. WebSockets) and shared storage for the admin pool.
- Pull logic and admin access currently run in the browser, so a player could edit them; move them server-side before a real launch.
